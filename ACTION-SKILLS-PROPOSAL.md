# Learning by doing: attributes and perks - revision 2

Discussion draft, 20 November 2026. Based on the installed V3.4 b10 skill descriptions, every available rank description and their configured effects. Covers all five attributes and 57 gameplay perks. **No runtime progression or skill points changed.**

## 1. Core rule: useful damage always counts; specialty earns extra

For each weapon-family combat perk, every eligible hit dealing real damage earns base practice. Aimed shots, combat reloads, power attacks, slows, bleeds, stuns and other matching specialties earn additional practice. A player can progress from ordinary damage without already owning the special effect. Utility/specialty perks use their relevant actions rather than receiving automatic XP from every kill.

Examples:

- Rifle hit -> Dead Eye base XP. Aimed rifle hit -> base plus aimed-hit bonus. Headshot/qualifying streak -> extra precision bonus. A completed combat reload -> a small reload award.
- Spear hit -> Spear Master base XP. Landed power attack -> extra. Fresh slow caused by that spear -> extra control practice. The native slow comes from power attacks; it is not exclusively a leg-hit effect. Leg-hit precision is an optional additional rule, not a claimed native mechanic.
- Knife hit -> Deep Cuts base XP. A landed power cut, new bleed wound and its real attributable damage -> bounded bonuses. Connected strikes can also train Whirlwind; hitting faster in itself is not automatically a new bleed wound.

Main weapon perks retain damage XP for their native weapon families. Attack-speed perks receive a smaller secondary damage share and emphasize landed hit sequences. Conditional specialties such as Hidden Strike and Run and Gun require their own condition; every ordinary kill does not automatically train every combat perk.

### 1.1 Reward rules

1. Damage XP uses actual health removed, capped to the target's remaining health. No misses, corpse damage, overkill, friendly damage or idle swinging.
2. Signature bonuses supplement damage; do not require an unlocked proc to earn the first rank. A power hit trains Spear Master before the slow chance is unlocked; the slow itself becomes an extra once available.
3. Reload practice requires a completed reload replacing ammunition spent in qualifying combat. Normalize by combat ammunition/capacity and cap its share. No XP for the reload key, empty reloads or repeated one-round cycles. Bow nocking/crossbow reloads follow the same principle, not a free repeatable action.
4. ADS means iron sights or an optic, not a requirement to find a scope. Holding aim alone earns none. Moving hip-fire is a different specialty and can train Run and Gun.
5. A slow, bleed, stun, knockdown or dismemberment must be attributed to the relevant player and eligible weapon. A refresh is not automatically a new event. Do not award continuous XP for every buff tick or for effects inflicted by allies/other weapons.
6. Multiple skill awards can arise from one action, but the parent attribute gets one budgeted contribution, not the sum of all child awards. A bonus cannot recursively generate another bonus.
7. Normalize shotgun pellets, automatic fire, DOT damage and multi-target blasts so weapon/event frequency alone cannot overwhelm progression. Direct and bleed damage share the victim's real damage budget.
8. Rates remain experimental. Reload/status events are bounded additions, not a requirement to play a narrow challenge checklist. Ordinary useful combat must remain worthwhile at every rank.

## 2. Attributes

| Attribute | Relevant activities |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | Club/sledge/shotgun combat, mining/chopping and useful loaded on-foot travel. |
| Perception | Rifles, spears, explosives, salvaging, tracking, treasure/loot searches and lockpicking. |
| Fortitude | Knuckles, machine guns, hunting/farming, genuine recovery and sustained exertion. |
| Agility | Knives, bows, handguns, evasive movement, meaningful jumps and enemy-aware stealth. |
| Intellect | Batons, robotics, engineering, medicine, mechanical vehicle work, real trade and quests. |

General-perk affinities: Light Armor -> Agility; Medium Armor -> Fortitude; Heavy Armor -> Strength; Master Chef -> Intellect; Living off the Land -> Fortitude; Lock Picking/Lucky Looter -> Perception. These are proposed practice affinities, not newly imposed native attribute gates.

## 4. Masteries and benefits that need redesign

Masteries should train advanced actions matching their individual rank benefits, rather than only receiving a generic category trickle. Their ordinary counterpart must train the next rank before its proc exists. Do not require harvesting steel before unlocking steel salvage, or having a deflection before learning it. Keep a baseline advanced-category route so injuries, infection and random loot are not mandatory training tools.

**Intellect Mastery requires explicit mode-dependent reward changes:** its book-finding rank can be useless if all relevant books are disabled, and its magazine-to-skill-point reward conflicts with removing points. Proposed replacements: Researcher rewards first successful complex engineering/medical/robotics work; Applied Knowledge gives a modest, bounded practice-learning benefit instead of point drops. Exact replacement effects/rates remain a separate design decision. They must work with books off and must not recursively multiply their own practice. Native book/point behavior can remain in vanilla-points mode.

Charismatic Nature has another deliberate exception: a solo training route through trader/community influence is possible, but native ally-only auras do not suddenly become solo buffs. If we want the perk to benefit a solo character, that needs an explicit modest solo effect or honest ally-only labeling. No new rescue quest system is being claimed.

The former draft showed Lucky Looter as one rank because it overlooked the inherited XML default. Corrected here to **five ranks**, matching the native perk default and its rank descriptions. All rank limits now honor explicit overrides and the inherited default.

## 5. Progression, preservation and play style

- Keep ordinary player XP/levels and existing crafting practice; the optional mode replaces spendable progression points. Weapon crafting and using that weapon remain related but separate practice systems.
- Preserve existing ranks, equipment, saves and purchased perks. Store practice by world and player. Archive unspent points if switching modes; do not silently delete/refund them or allow repeat-switch point duplication.
- Higher ranks cost more practice. Use real damage, outcome, resource and distance measurements; exact multipliers and hours-to-max require measurement rather than guesses.
- Retain native attribute gates initially, clearly display them and allow a bounded next-rank practice bank. No force to use every weapon: a dedicated knife user can eventually develop Agility.
- Starting routes must work with no perk ranks, no skill magazines and ordinary early equipment. A scoped rifle, AP ammo, stun baton or status proc may improve training but cannot become the sole entry route.
- Never make self injury, starvation, intentional infection, drowning, high falls or AFK actions the optimal grind. Legitimate recovery is allowed; provoked repeat-damage loops receive no repeated budget.
- Book sets/schematics retain their separate settings. Magazine loot bonuses do not become hidden prerequisites for action practice.

## 6. Implementation and evidence

1. Build a tracking-only ledger first. Compare base damage, aiming, real reloads and sourced effects on native and Arsenal weapons without changing ranks.
2. Validate native weapon/perk tags, head/leg/body hit information and modded model hitboxes. Treat missing hit-region data as ordinary damage XP, not a blocked skill.
3. Add reliable simple routes; then verify bleed/shock/slow attribution, projectile IDs, shotgun pellets, deployed robots and electrical traps.
4. Verify utility routes: injury provenance, item treatment, unique loot, lock attempts, trader reversals, armor mixtures, stealth and moving-combat conditions. Do not silently substitute every-kill XP for an unavailable unique event.
5. Check all 57 perks can start at zero and reach every native rank with books/points off. Resolve Intellect Mastery replacements and solo Charismatic Nature before declaring complete coverage.
6. Test persistence/migration, notification spam and rates, then offer optional Learn by doing mode. The current 0.1.38 gameplay remains unchanged by this document.

## 7. Recommended direction

Adopt **base weapon-damage XP plus description-driven bonus XP**. Prefer bonuses that reward normal good play, with small bounded reload/control awards. Keep every skill accessible before its special effect unlocks. Do not remove points from the live character until the complete optional system and migration are tested.
